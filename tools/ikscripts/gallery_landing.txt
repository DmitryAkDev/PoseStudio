# THE LANDING BOUNCE (2026-09-28): the Ground button drops a hovering figure, and one that comes
# down ON HER FEET absorbs the landing — the hips dip over the planted feet, the knees folding, and
# come back up — deeper the higher the drop. Purely aesthetic: the pose she ends in is the pose she
# was dropped in, exactly. `lift <m>` raises the figure, `ground` is the button, `wait` counts 60 Hz
# ticks: half a metre falls in 19 of them, and the dip is deepest some 8 later.

# 1. Dropped half a metre: mid-fall, at the deepest of the dip, and after.
view front
orbit 35 -8
lift 0.5
ground
wait 10
shot 501_falling
wait 17
report dip_50cm
expect euler.lShin.x > 20
expect euler.rShin.x > 20
expect meshlow > -3
shot 502_dip_50cm
wait 45
report after_50cm
expect euler.lShin.x == 0
expect euler.lThigh.x == 0
expect euler.lFoot.x == 0
expect meshlow > -1
expect meshlow < 1
shot 503_after_50cm

# 2. Dropped 5cm: a slight dip.
lift 0.05
ground
wait 11
report dip_5cm
expect euler.lShin.x > 5
expect euler.lShin.x < 30
shot 504_dip_5cm
wait 45
expect euler.lShin.x == 0

# 3. Dropped 2m: the deepest.
lift 2.0
ground
wait 49
report dip_2m
expect euler.lShin.x > 45
expect meshlow > -3
shot 505_dip_2m
wait 50
expect euler.lShin.x == 0
expect euler.lThigh.x == 0

# 4. A posed figure keeps her pose through it: an arm raised, a knee a little bent.
view front
orbit 35 -8
press lHand
dragv 0.25 0.45 40 20
release
lift 0.5
ground
wait 27
shot 506_posed_dip
wait 45
report posed_after
shot 507_posed_after
reset

# 5. NOT on her feet: kneeling, she lands on her knees and nothing bounces.
view right
press hip
dragv -0.22 -0.52 90 30
release
lift 0.5
ground
wait 27
report kneeling_landed
shot 508_side_kneeling_landed
wait 40
shot 509_side_kneeling_after
quit
