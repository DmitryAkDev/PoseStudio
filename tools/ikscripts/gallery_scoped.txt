# CTRL + DRAG (2026-09-26/28). On a limb or the head it is SCOPED: the grabbed chain alone follows
# the cursor — a hand pulled forward moves its arm and nothing else, the head bends the neck, a knee
# swings over its planted foot — and the numbers a user would see are what did NOT move: the hips,
# the feet, the chest, the other hand — 0.0mm, since a bone none of the solve's unknowns move does
# not move at all. On the BODY itself (the chest, the abdomen, the hips) it MOVES THE FIGURE as she
# is posed, off the floor's contacts, the user's pins holding.
#
# In `view right` the camera looks from +X: screen-left is her front.

# A hand pulled forward and up: the arm alone (the collar included), the hand on the cursor.
view right
cpress lHand
dragv -0.25 0.15 60 30
report scoped_hand
expect grab < 5
expect hips < 0.5
expect moved.chest_2 < 0.5
expect moved.lFoot < 0.5
expect moved.rHand < 0.5
expect steps < 1
shot 60_scoped_hand_side
release
reset

# The same hand pulled far beyond the arm's reach: the arm points at the cursor, she stands still.
view right
cpress lHand
dragv -0.30 0.90 60 30
report scoped_hand_far
expect hips < 0.5
expect moved.chest_2 < 0.5
expect moved.lFoot < 0.5
expect steps < 1
shot 60b_scoped_hand_far_side
release
reset

# The head pulled forward: the neck alone bends; the chest and everything below stay.
view right
cpress head
dragv -0.15 0 60 30
report scoped_head
expect head > 30
expect hips < 0.5
expect moved.chest_2 < 0.5
expect steps < 1
shot 61_scoped_head_side
release
reset

# THE FIGURE MOVE (2026-09-28): Ctrl + the BODY itself — the chest, the abdomen, the hips — moves
# the whole figure as she is posed: nothing is planted on the floor, no joint turns. The chest
# pulled forward and up: she is lifted off the floor and carried, feet and all.
view right
cpress chest_2
dragv -0.20 0.30 60 30
report figure_lifted
expect grab < 1
expect hips > 300
expect moved.lFoot > 300
expect moved.rFoot > 300
expect euler.lShin.x == 0
expect euler.abdomenLower.x == 0
expect euler.lShldr.z == 0
expect steps < 1
shot 62_ctrl_chest_lifted_side
release

# ... and the Ground button brings her back down (onto her feet: with the landing's bounce).
ground
wait 80
expect meshlow > -1
expect meshlow < 1
expect euler.lShin.x == 0
shot 62b_ctrl_lifted_grounded_side
# (Reset Pose takes the lift out of the pose and leaves the Ground button's drop in the model's
# transform — she stands 30cm under the floor — so Ground again: a sunk figure is lifted out at once.)
reset
ground
expect meshlow > -1
expect meshlow < 1

# The hips carried sideways along the floor: she slides as posed — no step, no lean.
view front
cpress hip
dragv 0.40 0 60 30
report figure_carried
expect grab < 1
expect moved.lFoot > 390
expect moved.head > 390
expect euler.lThigh.x == 0
expect euler.lThigh.z == 0
expect steps < 1
shot 63_ctrl_hips_carried_front
release
reset

# The hips pushed DOWN from standing: the floor stops her.
view front
cpress hip
dragv 0 -0.20 60 30
report figure_floor
expect hips < 1
expect euler.lShin.x == 0
expect meshlow > -1
release
reset

# A pinned hand HOLDS under the move: the body is carried toward it, the hand stays, its arm gives.
view front

expect moved.lFoot > 100
expect euler.lShin.x == 0

reset
